**Company Name:** San Diego Custom Painting (SDCP)
**Instagram:** @sdcustompainting
**Established:** 1998
**Type:** Family-owned, local San Diego painting contractor

**Mission Statement:** Our goal is to be the best experience a client will have when hiring a contractor.

## Brand Colors

- SDCP Blue: #1e396d
- Black: #000000

## Brand Tone

- **Confident, skilled, and professional** - We know our craft
- **Straightforward** - No fluff, no filler
- **Friendly but not casual** - Approachable yet professional

## Messaging Pillars

Use these themes consistently in all content:

1. **Quality craftsmanship** - Attention to detail, premium finishes
2. **Customer experience & trust** - Reliability, transparency, integrity
3. **Real before/after transformations** - Authentic results
4. **Behind-the-scenes professionalism** - Clean job sites, skilled crews
5. **Local San Diego community focus** - Family-owned, rooted in the community
6. **Cleanliness, reliability, punctuality** - Professional standards

## Words & Phrases to AVOID

- Cheap, lowest-cost, fast and cheap
- Overly technical jargon
- Over-promising words: "perfect," "never," "flawless," "guarantee"
- Casual/slang language
- Competitor comparisons

## Caption Style Guidelines

1. **Start with a strong hook** - Grab attention immediately
2. **Keep it concise** - 1-3 clean sentences for Instagram
3. **Focus on value or transformation** - What's the benefit?
4. **Professional and simple wording** - No fluff
5. **End with a CTA** - "Book a free estimate today." or similar

### Instagram vs Facebook

**Instagram:**
- Under 150 words
- Punchy, visual-focused
- NO hashtags in the caption (add separately)
- More transformation-focused

**Facebook:**
- Slightly longer, conversational
- Can include more context
- NO hashtags in caption
- More community/story-focused
